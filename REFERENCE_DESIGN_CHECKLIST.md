# Nepari-aligned visual regression checklist

Use this checklist with the screenshot suite. It records design direction, not copyrighted assets or copy.

- Persian content uses true RTL flow, appropriate alignment, and semantic icon direction.
- Estedad is loaded for Persian UI; headings are controlled and body copy remains readable.
- The header is compact and commerce-focused, with dense navigation, search, account, and cart access.
- The default palette stays light, agricultural, restrained, and based on semantic theme tokens.
- Hero composition balances a concise commercial message, product/agricultural visual, and clear actions.
- Category and product grids maintain consistent proportions, restrained corners, and minimal shadow.
- Sections use a regular spacing rhythm without giant gaps or cramped groups.
- Product cards keep imagery dominant, names compact, price/status clear, and actions easy to find.
- Footer information density supports shopping, service, contact, and trust without decorative excess.
- Desktop widths remain centered and deliberate; mobile retains readable two-column commerce where practical.
- Admin typography, colors, controls, and spacing remain coherent with the public agricultural identity.
- No accidental dark default, neon color, glassmorphism, excessive gradient, animation, or generic SaaS styling.
